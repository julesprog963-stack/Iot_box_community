POS receipts and kitchen tickets on your own IoT stack

Route customer receipts, manual reprints and preparation changes through Community IoT Box while retaining Odoo's configured printer as a controlled fallback.

Receipts and reprints

Automatic receipt printing plus manual printing and reprints with configurable copies and order references.

Kitchen preparation

New, changed and cancelled preparation lines are queued for the printer assigned to each preparation category.

Reliable fallback

RPC failures and unavailable IoT paths return control to the standard POS printer instead of silently losing output.

Workflow

Odoo POS  →  Community IoT queue  →  Agent  →  Receipt / kitchen printer

  • Independent receipt and preparation printer configuration.
  • Bounded payloads and 1–10 copies.
  • Multi-company device validation.
  • Compatible with existing ticket, cash drawer and label jobs.

Compatibility

Odoo 17, 18 and 19 Community or Enterprise · Odoo.sh and on-premise · Requires Point of Sale, community_iot_box and Community IoT Agent · Not available for Odoo Online/SaaS.

Envíe recibos, reimpresiones, folios y tickets de cocina del Punto de Venta mediante Community IoT Box.
